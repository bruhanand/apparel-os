# Needs coverage

> **Not ranked.** These notes describe the data KDPS sent. They decide nothing. A PRD or policy change they lead to needs an entry in [decisions.md](../decisions.md) first. See [README.md](README.md).

## 1. What this note is

- **The product owner's rule (4 Oct 2026).** KDPS will move fully to Apparel OS and may change how it works. Copying today's process is not a goal. But every need that KDPS's files and habits reveal must be covered.
- So this note reads every raw file, every sheet that does its own job, and every habit seen in the data, and asks three things: what job does it do today, what need sits behind it, and does the PRD, a KDPS policy or a design cover that need?
- Every one of the 136 raw files in [file-inventory.csv](file-inventory.csv) appears in at least one row below, by its exact path (section 8).
- The detail behind each row is in the topic notes it links to and in the folder READMEs under [data-from-kdps](../data-from-kdps/README.md).

### Outcomes

| Outcome | Meaning | What happens next |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Covered** | The need is met by the PRD, KDPS policies or designs named in the row | Build it as written |
| **Gap** | A real need with no home in the PRD, policies or designs | The product owner decides; see section 3. Nothing is built on a guess |
| **Retire habit** | Today's way breaks or bypasses a PRD rule. The PRD wins and KDPS changes the habit at the switch | The need behind it is still Covered or a Gap (the row says which); training and the switch plan carry the change |
| **Move data once** | A KDPS-made sheet that stops at the switch; its content is needed once, as opening data or history | Goes through the opening-data imports (`PRD-LIF-003` to `PRD-LIF-011`) with the clean-up in [data-quality-and-import-rules.md](data-quality-and-import-rules.md) |
| **Outside format** | An input from outside KDPS that keeps arriving: vendor PT files, brand offer mails, brand style lists, bank statements, the earlier POS's exports until each Store switches | The product supports it through saved import layouts (`PRD-IMP-001`, `PRD-IMP-003`) |
| **No need** | Nothing to carry over: duplicates, empty files, tool leftovers, and analyst values that must not become settings | Nothing; the row says why |

A row may carry two outcomes, joined with "+".

### How this changes the earlier notes

- A habit that breaks a PRD rule is not a clash with the PRD. It is a habit to retire. [prd-fit.md](prd-fit.md) uses the same words.
- Questions to KDPS are about business facts, policy values and moving data in. Questions that only ask how an old sheet works are dropped; [open-questions.md](open-questions.md) part C lists them with the reason.
- Values in KDPS sheets (the `P RATE` multiplier, GST slabs, carry-bag prices) and in earlier analyses (thresholds, costs, budgets) are evidence of a need, never a setting (`AGENTS.md`, "Never invent a value").

**What it says, in short**

- **Most needs are covered.** 231 of 309 rows name the PRD, policy or design that meets the need.
- **Many habits retire.** 78 rows are today's ways that the PRD replaces. The biggest:
  - stock transfers typed as sale lines;
  - stock found at an audit added as inward with no approval;
  - a price band typed into the colour column;
  - returns with no link to the original bill;
  - cash books with no counted cash;
  - per-person copies of the PT sheet with drifting formulas;
  - offers kept as free text and applied by hand;
  - 34 brand reports a month built by hand in Excel.
- **Offers carry most of the gaps.** 23 of the 56 Gap marks are in the offers part: quantity tiers, fixed prices, gifts at a token price, brand style lists and the brand-facing report.
- **Outside formats stay.** Vendor PT files (33 layouts), brand offer mails and style lists, the bank statement and, until each Store switches, the earlier POS's sales and stock exports. These need saved import layouts; [pt-layouts.json](pt-layouts.json) and [pos-export-layouts.json](pos-export-layouts.json) describe them.
- **KDPS's own sheets move once.** Masters, open supplier dues, open claims and the last stock list before each Store switches. Quantity at the switch comes only from the verified count (`PRD-LIF-027`).

### Gaps in this part

**Draft G-1 — Supplier cash discount and interest**
- **Need:** Suppliers give a cash discount (`CD` in `SUPPLIER DATA BASE`; `CD %` and `CD Amount` in `INVOICE DETAILS.`) and the party summary carries `Interest If Any`. The PRD has payment terms and no cash discount or interest (G-23).
- **Question:** Does Apparel OS record cash discount (rate, base, window) and interest as supplier terms, and apply them when a payment is proposed?
- **Options:** (a) Add cash discount and interest to the payment terms of `PRD-ORG-016` and apply them in payment runs (`PRD-PAY-005`) with Accounts approval. (b) Keep them as configured agreement terms with no automatic calculation; Accounts records the adjustment at payment. (c) Leave them to Tally.
- **Who decides:** Product owner for the rule; Accounts and the CA for values and posting (policies 1 and 9).
- **Would touch:** Finance and accounting, payables (`PRD-PAY-001`, `PRD-PAY-005`); Organisation (`PRD-ORG-016`); `POL-01.12`, `POL-01.14`; a posting map for discount received (`POL-09.11`).
- **Stage:** 1 (terms record), 2 (match to invoice), 5 (payment runs).

**Draft G-2 — Claim kinds and return reasons**
- **Need:** The claims workbook lists 7 `DN TYPE` values and 5 `REASON OF GR` values (G-34, G-37). `PRD-OFR-018` names shortage, damage, price differences, promotional funding and display support. `STAFF SALARY REIMBURSEMENT`, `MONTHLY TARGET INCENTIVE`, `FURNITURE CLAIM` and `EOSS CREDIT NOTES` are defined but unused on rows; `WRONG INVOICE` claims are settled bill to bill.
- **Question:** Which claim kinds and return reasons does the claims register hold, and does the PRD fix the list or does the Organisation configure it?
- **Options:** (a) Extend the kinds in `PRD-OFR-018`. (b) A configurable list of kinds and reasons, with the PRD naming the minimum. (c) Keep the PRD's kinds; route staff reimbursement and target incentive through partner commission (`PRD-PAY-012`) and HR.
- **Who decides:** Product owner; Accounts and the Brand manager (which kinds exist in practice).
- **Would touch:** Offers, prices and supplier returns (`PRD-OFR-018`, `PRD-OFR-019`); `PRD-DMG-009`; `PRD-PAY-012`; `POL-01.10`.
- **Stage:** 3.

**Draft G-3 — Promo, gift-with-purchase and packaging goods**
- **Need:** Promo goods (duffel bags, backpacks, trolleys) are billed with the goods at about MRP, tracked as `... PROMO` brand variants and a `PROMO` booking status; 11 offers are `GWP` (G-28).
- **Question:** How are promo, gift-with-purchase and packaging goods received, valued, stocked and issued?
- **Options:** (a) A non-merchandise item class kept out of stock value, dead stock and margin. (b) Ordinary SKUs with a flag and a quantity-tracked profile (`POL-04.07`). (c) Configuration only, no PRD rule.
- **Who decides:** Product owner; KDPS Owner; Accounts (cost).
- **Would touch:** Merchandise and identifiers (`PRD-MER-004`, `PRD-MER-014`); `PRD-OFR-001`; reports (`PRD-EXC-006`, `PRD-NAV-001`).
- **Stage:** 2 (receiving), 4 (issue at the till).

**Draft G-4 — Supplier invoice or claim addressed to another legal entity**
- **Need:** A register is addressed to Om Ganpati Enterprises for a KDPS Store, an invoice print to D D Developers, and credit notes land in the wrong entity's ledger and are patched by remark (G-42, G-62, G-67).
- **Question:** What does the product do when a supplier invoice or credit note names a different legal entity or registration from the one mapped to the receiving unit?
- **Options:** (a) Invoice capture compares the bill-to registration with the receiving unit's mapping, raises an Accounts-owned exception, and posts only to the matching entity's book. (b) Allow assigning the document to the named entity when it is a legal entity of the Organisation, with inter-entity handling for the goods. (c) Out of scope; Accounts handles it in Tally.

- **Would touch:** Receiving (`PRD-REC-010`); Finance and accounting (`PRD-PAY-001`, `PRD-TAX-002`); Organisation (`PRD-ORG-005`, `PRD-ORG-020`); `PRD-TRF-004`; `PRD-FRN-006`; `POL-10.01`, `POL-10.08`, `POL-12.01`.

### Gaps in this part

**Draft M-1 — Gift-with-purchase and packaging items**
- **Need:** Carry bags, gift trolleys and promotional items leave stock as lines at 100% discount or token prices, mixed with sales (rows M-38, M-33).
- **Question** for the product owner: Does the PRD name a class of stocked non-merchandise items (packaging and gifts) that are issued at no charge, kept out of stock value, dead stock and margin, and who may give a free gift?
- **Options:** (a) a non-merchandise item class with its own issue movement and expense posting; (b) ordinary SKUs flagged as gifts, given through an approved offer (buy-X-get-Y) with the funded cost recorded; (c) leave it to configuration per tracking profile and keep sale lines.
- **Who decides:** product owner (the rule); KDPS Owner and Accounts (cost treatment, and whether a bag is ever charged).
- **Would touch:** Merchandise and identifiers (`PRD-MER-004`, `PRD-MER-014`); Offers, prices and supplier returns (`PRD-OFR-001`, `PRD-OFR-006`); Counter sales and payments (`PRD-POS-003`); Exceptions, reports and planning (`PRD-EXC-006`, `PRD-EXC-008`); new bullets in Merchandise and identifiers and in Stock and warehouse control (issue for consumption).
- **Stage:** item class stage 1; issue and billing stage 4; margin reports stage 5.

**Draft M-2 — Freight on transfers**
- **Need:** A carrier's "to pay" charge for one load is shared between two Stores by hand, with no record of who pays or where the cost goes (rows M-53, M-18).
- **Question** for the product owner: Is freight on a transfer an expense of the receiving Store, an addition to stock cost or a head-office expense, and how is one charge for a load that serves two Stores shared?
- **Options:** (a) an expense of the receiving Store, allocated by Store and brand; (b) an addition to stock cost through a later approved cost adjustment (strains "no markup" in `PRD-TRF-012`); (c) a head-office expense shared on a base Accounts sets.

### Gaps in this part

**Draft O-1 — Offers that depend on how many units are bought**
- **Need:** A discount that grows with the count of eligible units: 51 `EOSS` lines in `BRAND OFFERS.xlsx`, Linen Club `BUY 2 GET 750/- OFF` slabs, Spykar denim `BUY 3 … GET RS.2000`, `B2 or more @ 40%` in the Allen Solly emails, Mufti, Killer, Libas (O-03, O-11 to O-14, O-16).
- **Question:** Should the PRD add a minimum-quantity condition to percentage and amount-off offers, and say whether the higher rate applies to every qualifying unit or only from the Nth?
- **Options:** (a) A "quantity tier" on percentage and amount-off offers, with "every unit" or "from the Nth unit" chosen per offer at approval. (b) Keep four kinds but give each a minimum-quantity condition; each tier is one offer and `PRD-OFR-021` picks the best. (c) Leave tiers out; the brand's tier is entered as a manual discount, which needs authority and a reason on every bill (`PRD-POS-003`).
- **Who decides:** Product owner for the PRD change, with the Brand manager. Each offer's reading is set at approval by the authorised approver.
- **Would touch:** `PRD-OFR-001`, `PRD-OFR-002`, `PRD-OFR-021`; shared-calculations 5.3 and 5.5 (`GC7-9`). [prd-fit.md](prd-fit.md) candidate 2.
