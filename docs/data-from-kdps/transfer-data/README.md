Up: [data-from-kdps](../README.md). Notes: [docs/data-notes](../../data-notes/README.md).

## What this folder is

- Three phone photos, sent by WhatsApp on 25 Jun 2026 (16:07 and 16:08), of the paper that goes with stock moved by road from Ranchi to the Deoghar and Hazaribagh Stores: two transporter gate passes, one KDPS delivery challan, and one transporter consignment note.
- Made by KDPS staff (photos) from paper made by the transporters and by KDPS. They relate to the data-request checklist item "WH TO STORE TRANSFER FORMAT" (`../Q&A-req-recieved/MOM_S.xlsx` marks it "EXPLAINED", with the provider Debanjan). The photos may be what was sent to show the format (guess).
- This is paper only: no system record of the dispatch or of the receipt is in the folder. A stock transfer in Apparel OS keeps dispatch, transport evidence and destination count as separate records (`PRD-TRF-011`, `PRD-TRF-013`, `PRD-TRF-014`), and statutory movement documents follow ownership, entity and registration rules (`PRD-TRF-023`).
- 3 files. Papers dated 16 Jun 2026 and 23 Jun 2026.

## What the three papers show together

- Stock reaches Deoghar by a road carrier on a gate pass per consignment. Freight is paid at destination ("to pay") and split between the Stores by hand.
- A KDPS delivery challan exists for the Hazaribagh dispatch with category-level lines and no tax. The two Deoghar gate passes have no KDPS document in the photos.
- The receiving count is a handwritten tally of pieces by document number, not a scan, and it is not signed in the photos.
