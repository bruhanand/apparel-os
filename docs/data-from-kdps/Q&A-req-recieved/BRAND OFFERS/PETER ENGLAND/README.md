# PETER ENGLAND

Raw files here are not in git (see the repository .gitignore); only this README is.

Up: [BRAND OFFERS](../README.md)

## What this folder is

Three phone screenshots (JPEG) of emails about Peter England (PE) offers. Two are Peter England emails. The third, `PE MAY.jpeg`, is not a Peter England email at all (see below). Not made by KDPS. Files saved on 13 Jun 2026 (file dates 03:05 to 03:07).

- `PE MAY.jpeg` (953 x 1600 pixels, 85,795 bytes) is byte-identical to `LP MAY.jpeg` in the [LOUIS PHILLIPE](../LOUIS%20PHILLIPE/README.md) folder (same MD5 checksum). It shows the Louis Philippe offer table. So there is no Peter England offer for May in this folder. The file was probably copied here by mistake (guess).
- `PE JUNE.jpeg` and `PE ABV OFFER.jpeg` are 720 x 1600 pixels each. They are Peter England offer tables.
- No sender or recipient names are copied from the screenshots. Where a screenshot shows them, they stay out of this README.

- Row 1 lists 43 category words. They look like system category codes with shortened or misspelled names (for example `Ktregg`, `Lnkurst`, `Msungla`, `Tproduc`, `Waistct`) (inferred). They are not a style list.
- Row 4 `S&B` is probably suits and blazers (guess), with a pocket-square category spelled `Pocksqa`. "Flat price Off" with six figures (599, 1999, 2999, 5999, 8999, 12999) does not say whether each figure is a selling price or an amount off, or which figure belongs to which style. The style-code attachment would decide.
- Rows 6 and 7: `PE TR MBO ABV` is not expanded. MBO is the PRD word for a multi-brand outlet, so these may be bill-value offers for multi-brand Stores (guess). The two slabs are Rs 1,000 off at Rs 4,999 and Rs 1,999 off at Rs 9,999. The email does not say if "shopping of" is measured on MRP or net.
- Rows 8 to 10 `PE GST benefit` (11.02%, 6.25%, FLAT): not explained. They carry the same dates as the offers. They may describe a support amount that the brand gives to cover a GST change (guess). They are not customer-facing offers.
