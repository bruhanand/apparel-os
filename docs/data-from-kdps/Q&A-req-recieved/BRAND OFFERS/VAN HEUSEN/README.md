## Words as written

- Sub-brand codes: `HOVH`, `VS`, `VD`, `VX`, `VF`, `VW`. They are not expanded in the emails. In `SUPPLIER BRAND DETAILS.xlsx` each of `VH`, `VF`, `VX`, `VD`, `VS` and `VW` is a brand name mapped to MADURA PVT LTD (`VAN HEUSEN` maps to MADURA PVT LTD and `VAN HEUSEN WOMENS` to ADITYA BIRLA LIFESTYLE BRANDS LIMITED). `HOVH` is not expanded either.
- `AMM list`: a style list (not sent). Column `Remarks` says "As per AMM list" on most rows.
- `Abv` in "B2 & Abv @ 30%" probably means "above": two or more pieces (inferred).
- `Flat` followed by a number: a flat price (for example `Flat - 4999`). `Flat @ 40%`: a flat percentage off.
- `B1`, `B2`: buy 1, buy 2.

## `VH APRIL 1.jpeg` in detail

- A note: instead of "750 additional shopping" the dealer can give "750 off". Two small tables follow.

| Table | Row 1 | Row 2 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Revised - New | Shop for 7999 and get 750 Off | Shop for 4999 and get 750 off |
| Existing | Shop for 7999 and get 750 additional shopping | Shop for 4999 and get 750 additional shopping |

  So the earlier offer paid the benefit as extra shopping (a voucher, inferred) and the revised one gives an instant discount. The revised rows match rows 16 and 17 of the main table.

- Who funds each offer.
