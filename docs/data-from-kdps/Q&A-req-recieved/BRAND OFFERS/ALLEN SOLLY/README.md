## What this folder is

Three phone screenshots (JPEG, 720 x 1600 pixels) of emails that the Allen Solly (AS) trade team sent to KDPS staff. Each lists the brand offers for one month of 2026: April, May and June. They were not made by KDPS. The files were saved on 13 Jun 2026 (file dates, 02:39 to 02:40).

- The screenshots are Android Gmail views of brand emails. The sender is a trade executive of Allen Solly (an Aditya Birla Group brand, shown by the logo). The sender's name, a mobile number and internal mailing-list addresses are visible. None of them are copied here.
- The same three emails were also cut into three PNG crops of the tables, kept one level up as `ALLEN SOLLY-1.png`, `ALLEN SOLLY-2.png` and `ALLEN SOLLY-3.png` (see [BRAND OFFERS](../README.md)). The crops add nothing new.
- No machine-readable version exists. The lists of style codes that the offers refer to (see "Words as written") were not sent.
- Madura-brand offers were marked "MADURA PENDING" in `MOM_S.xlsx` item 3 (see [Q&A-req-recieved](../../README.md)). These screenshots, with the Louis Philippe, Peter England and Van Heusen ones, are how KDPS supplied them.

## Words as written in the emails

The emails do not explain these terms. Guesses are marked.

- `B1`, `B2`: buy 1, buy 2. `B2 or more` means two or more pieces.
- `B1G1`: buy 1 get 1.
- `@20%`: percentage off. The emails do not say whether the higher percentage in `B1@20% B2@30%` applies to every piece or only to the second.
- `AMM` (column `Type`): not defined. Offers of this type are tied to "AMM style codes" (a list of styles). Probably a list of styles eligible for the percentage offers (guess).
- `ABV` (column `Type`): bill-value offers ("Shop for 5999 and get 1000rs off"). The LP and PE emails also use ABV for the same kind of offer. Not expanded (guess: a bill-value slab).
- `NOD` and `HOAS`: used in "HOAS NOD codes", "All NOD KIDS Style codes" and "Men's NOD style codes". Not defined. Probably NOD marks a style list that is separate from AMM (guess).
- `Promo` or `PROMO`: a gift or bag at a token price against a spend threshold. Some rows add "wherever stock has been sent" or "Selected partners, wherever stock is available". The offer therefore depends on stock being present.

## What changed from April to May to June

- April to May: the "Suits & Blazers" row (`B1@30%`) became "Blazers & Jackets" with the same slab. Two rows were added: "Buy 2 T-shirt and get 500/- off" (men's T-shirts, type AMM) and "Shop for 14999 and get 2000rsoff" (ABV, HOAS NOD codes). The separate "Womens Day Offers" table is gone, but the same flat 40% on women's AMM bags stays in table 1. The order of rows changed. The start moved from "1st April" to "1st May".
- May to June: one extra men's flat `B1@30%` for 10th to 18th June. The June email does not say whether the May list stays in force beside it (the May list says "Till further communication").
- The April and May emails say the offers run until further communication. They never say the earlier month is withdrawn.

## What the screenshots do not say

- Which styles are in the AMM and NOD lists. The lists were not sent.
- Whether `B1@20% B2@30%` gives 30% on every piece or only on the second, and which piece is free in `B1G1`.
- Whether "Shop for 5999" is measured on MRP or on the bill after other discounts, and whether the threshold is per bill or per brand.
- Whether percentage offers (AMM) and bill-value offers (ABV) combine. They apply to different style lists (AMM against NOD), so they may not meet on the same piece (guess).
- Who funds each offer (the brand or KDPS) and how the brand pays its share.
- Whether a gift or bag offer applies when the gift is out of stock.
