2 · Menus by persona

What each of the PRD’s 14 personas sees in the sidebar, built from the PRD’s “Work supported” column. A person can hold several personas: they get one combined menu, one home with a block per persona, and one My work. A persona grants nothing by itself; only role assignments do, each inside its own scope, and the preparer still cannot approve their own record. Below each persona: its group and the KDPS template that serves it (policy 2). Details: personas.md. Each menu lists every section where the grid in 3a gives that persona more than none; Home appears where the grid gives it (the cashier, salesperson and EBO staff land on the till or the portal). Portal and Self-service are shells: portal pages sit under the section that holds them (Uploads under External sales; Petty cash and cash deposit under Money). Menu names are working labels. PRD-UXP-004 lists Store capabilities and areas, not menu labels (DEC-056).

Derived, not decided. The grid follows the PRD’s “Work supported” column. Real grants, scopes and limits are KDPS values set under policy 2. Menus in section 2 list every section where a persona’s rung is above none (PRD-ACS-002, PRD-ACS-003).
Approvals are permissions. Approve PT, approve transfer and approve differences are checked against the approve rung plus the policy 2 limit, never a role name. The preparer can never approve. A cell marked “policy 2” has no decided approver: who holds it, and up to what limit, is OPEN (KDPS Owner; blocks stage 1 live approvals). The grid gives no persona a default approve rung for PT approval, damage confirmation, stock adjustments or supplier-return steps. Baseline (DEC-105): the Owner template approves write-offs, disposals and count differences above the Store manager’s tolerance, within limits KDPS sets; the KDPS Owner confirms.
Self-service via role assignment. Own-record self-service (attendance, targets, payslips) is granted only through an assignment of its own self-service role, scoped to the person's own records (`DEC-041`, `DEC-100`, `PRD-ACS-022`).
Several personas. One person can hold several rows. The menu is the union; each action is checked inside one role assignment’s scope, and the reason names any gap (“Your Store manager assignment covers BLR01 only”).
Partner users. They hold Store personas on their own Stores only; their statements come from a permission. There is no separate partner persona.
