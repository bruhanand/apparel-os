2 · Menus by persona

What each of the PRD’s 14 personas sees in the sidebar, built from the PRD’s “Work supported” column. A person can hold several personas: they get one combined menu, one home with a block per persona, and one My work. A persona grants nothing by itself; only role assignments do, each inside its own scope, and the preparer still cannot approve their own record. Below each persona: its group and the KDPS template that serves it (policy 2). Details: personas.md.
